\section{Data Release Processing} \label{sec:drp}

Data Release Processing (DRP) is the data pipeline that produces the calibrated images, detection catalogs, and derived data
products defined in section \S TBD, using the LSST Science Pipelines (citation). DP2 was processed at the United States Data Facility (USDF) at SLAC, with pilot runs at the National Institute of Nuclear and Particle Physics (IN2P3) computing center.

The processing campaign consists of four major stages: (1) single-frame processing, (2) calibration, (3) multi-visit processing, (4) variable and transient source processing.
Data quality and key metrics for verification and validation are actively monitored during each stage, and comprehensive verification and validation assessments are completed before processing the next stage.

This section describes the high-level algorithms, configurations, and verification metrics for each stage, including changes and improvements from DP1 (citation).
Quantitative performance figures appear in \secref{sec:performance}.

\subsection{Calibration} \label{sec:drp-calibration}

Stars detected across visits are matched to build a consistent calibration sample.
Initial solutions from the reference catalog and PSFEx are refined iteratively using Piff, gbdes, and FGCM.
The Monster reference catalog provides astrometric and photometric anchors; commissioning constraints include a short temporal baseline and limited $u$-band depth.

\begin{itemize}
\item Star matching across visits; iterative refinement after initial PSFEx and reference-catalog solutions.
\item Monster reference catalog role; commissioning constraints (short baseline, shallow $u$-band).
\item What improved for DP2: differential chromatic refraction (DCR) in astrometry, as-built throughputs in FGCM, and full focal-plane PSF spatial structure.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Variable \& Transient Source Processing} \label{sec:drp-variabletransient}

This stage produces difference images and catalogs, multi-epoch forced photometry, and solar system tables.
Templates are drawn from template coadds; difference imaging runs at full focal-plane scale.
DRP prioritizes data quality over the latency constraints of Prompt Processing and the operational pixel embargo \citep{RTN-011}.
