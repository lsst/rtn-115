\subsubsection{Star--Galaxy Separation}
DP2 provides several different star--galaxy classifiers within the Object catalog.
We use objects located within a $1\deg$ radius of the Milky Way satellite Virgo III located at (R.A., decl.) = $(186\fdg3, 4\fdg4)$ in the Rubin `Cosmic Treasure Chest' (M49) First Look field to observe the performance of the various star--galaxy classifiers around Milky Way halo systems.
Although the depth of the M49 field is not representative of the other DP2 fields, we see a similar trend in the performances of the various star--galaxy classifiers around other Milky Way halo objects such as the Aquarius II dwarf galaxy (SV1000 field) and AM1 globular cluster (EDFS field).
\figref{fig:sg_classifier_vs_maglim} shows the performance of various star--galaxy separation cuts.
In the top panel, for each star--galaxy cut we plot the cumulative $g$-band median signal to noise ratio (SNR) of all the selected ``stars" as a function of magnitude limit.
The bottom panel shows the fraction of objects that pass the cut as a function of magnitude limit.
Note that \texttt{refExtendedness} and \texttt{\{band\}\_size\_extendedness} are binary classifiers, while \texttt{griz\_model\_extendedness} is a floating point classifier.
The behavior of the classifiers stars to diverge around $g\sim23$.
The median SNR of the most conservative $\texttt{griz\_model\_extendedness} < 0.25$ cut remains the highest at faint magnitudes as expected, while the \texttt{refExtendedness} classifier selects the most number of objects at faint magnitudes.
We expect therefore expect the most complete star sample using \texttt{refExtendedness}, at the cost of heavy galaxy contamination at faint magnitudes.
We therefore recommend the use of \texttt{refExtendedness} in studies where the completeness of the stellar sample is of higher priority than galaxy contamination.
Conversely, if the purity of the stellar sample is of paramount importance, we recommend the use of the floating point \texttt{griz\_model\_extendedness} with suitable tuning of the floating point cut to minimize galaxy contamination.
