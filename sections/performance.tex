\subsection{Stellar Photometry}
Since most of the DP2 footprint area was observed multiple times, we can use the photometric repeatability of bright (i.e., SNR$>200$), isolated, unresolved objects to assess the quality of our photometry at the visit-level.
Repeatability is measured by calculating the standard deviation of multiple measurements of the same object across all visits that cover that object. 
To make this calculation computationally feasible, however, this repeatability measurement is performed at the tract-level: the same set of objects are identified in each visit that cover a given tract, and the between-visit standard deviation of the multiple photometric measurements of those objects is calculated.
